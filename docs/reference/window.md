# The window

The window's layout (rail, tiles, Chat column), workspaces and agents, the Board, PR review, and workflows and skills.

## Layout

The editor, normal project tree, native Git window and terminal are the main workspace. **The rail** runs down the window's left edge from the toolbar to the status bar (52 px), in place of the old workspace strip and stripe. Top to bottom: **Abe** (the repo's main checkout, where its Abe chat lives; Abe's face `A`, always first, never closes; the cards' teal dot on its top right corner while its Abe chat asks you something), a tile per open card workspace in the order it opened, a tile per prototype (below), a dashed **+**, then the repo's places at its foot: **Board**, **Boxes**, **Pipelines**. The order holds: a state lights a tile, never moves it, and a done card's tile stays until you close it. A card's tile (36 px) shows two letters from its title on the card's own colour: a leading `As a … I can` and small words (in, with, an, the, a, of, to, for, on, and, my) skipped, the first letters of the first two words (one word: its first two letters); two open tiles never share them, the later one taking its first word's first two letters, then its first letter and the next consonant. Its colour is one of seven, none a state's (teal, amber, green, red): the first no open tile has, kept while its tile is open (`<state>/vimstorm/rail-hues.json`). Under the letters the **stage bar**, five bars (Spec · Build · Review · PR · Done, as the StageRail): the stages done in its tint, the one it is on in its state's colour (amber running, teal needs you, dim idle, red failed; pulsing while it launches; no stage yet: on the first), a done card all five green; needs you also puts a teal dot on its top right corner, a ☁ on its top left when it runs on a box. Nothing blinks. The hover card says its letters on its colour, `#id`, the title, the stage bar with its stage and state word (needs you's mark a teal `?` circle, never ◆), where it runs and the page it is on. The selected tile has a white pill at the rail's left edge (a short one on hover). When the cards don't fit, the ones shown keep the selected one in view and a line under them says `N below` (in teal, a `?` circle before it, when one out of view needs you). A click goes to a tile. A workspace tile's right-click (or ⌘⏎ with the pointer over it) is its menu: Story (its Card page, ⌃3), Open in Finder, Stop the session… (asked, Cancel the default; the workspace stays), then Delete Working Files… and Close the workspace (not on Abe). **+** is a menu: New card (the Board on its New card dialog, also File › New Workspace…) · New prototype… (its words asked, then `abe prototype start`; it lands on the new prototype's tile).

A **prototype** (a throwaway `proto/…` branch, never pushed; `/abe:prototype` in the Abe chat, a card's Prototype… on the Board, or New prototype… on **+**) has its own tile after the card workspaces until it is discarded: 30 px with a dashed edge, two letters as a card's (a card's prototype on the card's colour, an idea grey) and one bar under them, green running, amber starting, red failed, dim stopped; a teal dot while its chat asks you something. ⌘↑ / ⌘↓ go round it with the workspaces. Its hover says its words, branch, state and where it runs (this Mac, or ☁ its box with the port forwarded); its right-click (⌘⏎ over it) is Make card…, Open in browser, Restart app, Stop app, Discard…. Its pages: **Preview** ⌃1 (its app live in VimStorm, a WebKit view reaching only the prototype's proxy, under a URL bar: host and path, reload, ↗ open in the browser, the viewport Fit · 1280 · 768 · 390, a device frame at 390; ⏎ gives the app the keys, Esc always gives them back; while it starts, Starting the app… with each step; a failed start its last lines, ⏎ asks the prototype to fix it, `R` restarts; a stopped one its last screenshot, dimmed, ⏎ starts it again), **Code** ⌃2 (its diff by file, `l ö` the files, ⏎ opens one in the editor, and its screenshots) and **Logs** ⌃3 (the app's output, then the hosts the proxy refused). On every page `r` reload, `R` restart the app, `v` the next viewport, `o` open in the browser, `m` Make card… (asks its session), `s` stop the app, `d` Discard… (asked, Cancel the default), ⌘⏎ its menu. The Chat column there is the prototype's session. The header row names it, its branch and state, its pages and Make card · Open in browser · Restart app · Discard…. A launch on this machine (the Board's Launch box, the Abe chat) puts its workspace on the rail at once, its stage bar pulsing; moved to before its worktree is there, it shows the Board on its card: `Launching #N… its workspace is on the way`.

Right of the rail, under the toolbar, the **header row** names the tile: on Abe its face, `Abe` and `main checkout · <branch>`; on a card `#id`, its title, its stage rail, its stage and state word; on Board, Boxes or Pipelines the place and the repo. On a workspace it ends with its **pages** (a click or the key): **Code** ⌃1 (the editor: the tree and the splits, over its bottom panel), **Run** ⌃2 (the card's run: `l ö` its steps, `gg` / `G` the first / last; the step shown scrolls with ⌃E / ⌃Y a line, ⌃D / ⌃U half the pane, PgDn / PgUp or Space / ⇧Space the pane, Home / End its top / foot) and **Card** ⌃3 (the story as a page: its description, acceptance criteria and comments with a composer, and its actions Comment ⏎, Move…, Assign…, Launch again…, Stop the session; it replaces the story dialog). Abe has no card: its Run and Card are dim. Back on a workspace, its page shows again (kept across launches, `<state>/vimstorm/workspace-pages.json`); one never shown lands on Run while its card runs or needs you, else on Code. A file opened in it shows Code.

Code's **bottom panel** sits under the tree and the editor, full width: tabs **Terminal** (the workspace's shells, its own shell tabs after them) · **Git** (the Git tool window: Local Changes · Log) · **Problems** (the workspace's errors and warnings by file), then ⤢ and Hide at its right end. One tab shows at a time; drag its top edge for its height; ⤢ (or ⌥⌘⏎ with its keys) fills the middle with it, again restores. ⌘3 is its **Git** (shown on Git with the keys at its height; on the Terminal or Problems: switched to Git; on Git without the keys: the keys; with them: hidden; outside a git repository Git is passed over, Problems instead); ⌘T and ⌃` its Terminal, the same way (on Git: switched to the Terminal); ⌘J shows it on its last tab / hides it. With its keys ⌃⌘← / ⌃⌘→ (or ⌥⌘← / ⌥⌘→) are the Terminal's previous / next shell tab, Git's previous / next tab (in a diff: its files). From Run or Card these keys show Code with it. Each workspace keeps its panel: shown or not, its tab and its height (with its page in `workspace-pages.json`); a new workspace starts with it hidden. It shows on Code only. Only the middle changes between tiles and pages: the title bar, the status line, the rail and Chat stay. On the Board, Boxes, Pipelines, Run and Card the status bar's mode says `ABE · BOARD`, `ABE · BOXES`, `ABE · PIPELINES`, `ABE · RUN`, `ABE · CARD`, on a prototype's pages `ABE · PREVIEW`, `ABE · CODE`, `ABE · LOGS`; the toolbar's context line is the workspace's on its pages (Run and Card too) and Abe's on the places.

The rail's keys are the window's, taken before any surface (not while a popup, menu, dialog or Command mode is up): ⌘↑ / ⌘↓ the tile above / below in its group, round again past either end: on a workspace the workspaces (Abe, the cards, then the prototypes), on the Board, Boxes or Pipelines those three; ⌘← down to the places (the Board); ⌘→ back up to the workspaces (from the Board the selected card's, else the one you were in); ⌘§ Abe, again back; ⌃1–⌃3 the pages (the table below). Every ⌘ move (and Open card to a workspace's Card page) lands with the keys where they were as the tile was left (a workspace: on the page it shows), kept across launches in `rail-memory.json`: in Chat's composer (Claude Code's terminal when the tile's Chat is in that mode) or on the page (the Board's, Boxes' or Pipelines' list, Run, the Card page, the code where nvim had them). A first visit lands in Chat (on the page when Chat is hidden there), a maximized Chat always takes them; ⌃1–⌃3 give them to the page. Esc in Chat gives them back to the page.

The **Chat column** is the right island beside the code on every tile (420 px, drag its left gap between 320 and 640; the width is the tile's), full height from the header row to the status bar; the tree, the editor and the pages sit left of it. Its header: **Chat | Terminal**, ⋯ (Drag Out ⌘⇧D, Hide) and ×. Chat mode is the agent's **rendered Chat**: the pinned session's transcript as turns, changes first (a changes bar `N files changed` with Keep · Revert · Open all, a diff card per file, folded step rows, the agent's prose, stage dividers), under a two-line header (`#2` and the story title, the box's cloud or `Local sandbox`; the StageRail, the state word in its hue, the time in it, `N subagents`). Chat takes the keys in its composer (⌘4, ⌃⌘←, every rail move, `:chat`, a session pinned or launched, a new conversation; an open question card waits: `Tab to answer`); Esc gives them to the transcript (Esc there: to the page): `l ö` the parts, `gg` / `G` first / last, ⏎ opens the part (a diff card: its file at the change; a step: its output), `u` reverts a file (on the bar: the turn; asked first), `y` copies the part (a step's output, a diff card's path, the turn's answer), ⌘⏎ the session's actions. ⌘F (or `/` in the transcript) finds in it: a bar at its top, `3 of 12`, every match marked, ignoring case (your messages, the agent's prose, step rows; a folded turn's rows open as you reach one), ⏎ / ↓ the next, ⇧⏎ / ↑ the one before, Esc closes it, the keys back where they were; `n` / `N` in the transcript go on with the last text. The mouse selects the transcript's text as every Abe view's ([Mouse and menus](editor.md#mouse-and-menus)); a message or answer shows a copy button at its top right on hover, a code block at its top left. When the session asks, its question shows as a **question card**: the question itself first, bold, the rest of what the agent wrote folded under it (`m` or a click on `▸ More context` unfolds it), then the rows (1–9 pick, the recommended one first, ⏎ confirm, `y` accept the recommended, `o` your own words, Esc later: a ◆ pill stays; one that takes several answers has a box per row: Space or a digit ticks, ⏎ sends the ticked ones); a grill round shows one question at a time with its place (`2 of 3`; Tab / ⇧Tab move), sent as one message. The **composer** at its foot is a plain macOS text field, its placeholder saying whom it goes to (`Message #12 Cart total rounds wrong…`, `Ask Abe · make a card · /abe:…`, `Message the prototype…`; typing never waits on Abe; selection, ⌥ / ⌘ arrows, ⌘Z, any paste whole): it grows with its lines to 40 % of the column, then scrolls; ⏎ send, ⇧⏎ new line, `/` the commands (`/override` `/compact` `/clear` `/model` `/effort`), then every skill the session's Claude Code loads, filtered as you type (`/tri` finds `/abe:triage`), each with whose it is and what it does on one line (`/abe:triage  Abe’s · …`: Abe's as `/abe:<name>`, the Abe chat's own `/abe:install` … there, the repo's `.claude/skills` as `/<name>`, yours only with My skills on top), ten rows at a time; ⏎ / Tab puts it in the field and the next ⏎ sends it as text, a dim implicit-context chip (the selection or the file and caret line; ⌫ drops it for one message; none on `!` shell input, which goes as typed), `opus · medium` and queued messages. ⌘V of an image (a screenshot) or of files, or files dropped on it, puts them in it as chips (a click drops one); they go with the message as `@path`s Claude Code reads. Right above it the **status line** says what the agent does now and for how long (`Thinking`, `Reading cart.ts`, `Waiting on 2 subagents`, `Waiting on you` on a question or a dialog, `Idle · your turn` once a turn simply ended, `Your turn · 2 subagents working` while its background subagents still work) and at its right `N subagents`: Tab (or a click) unfolds them, each with its task, its last tool and its time; `l ö` move, ⏎ goes to its Agent call in the transcript, `o` opens its own transcript, Esc folds them. A teammate's session is read-only; with no session ⏎ is Launch agent. In a repo's main checkout with no story the column is the repo's **Abe chat** (`:chat`; header Abe's face, `Abe · <repo>` and `Local · no sandbox`, the checkout dim under it; Abe's face faint behind its transcript; it is the Abe tile's Chat: ⌘§, the key left of 1, goes there and again back): unsandboxed on this machine, it answers about cards, makes cards, starts workflows on one card or many, and runs Abe's skills as `/abe:<name>` (`/abe:install`, `/abe:triage`, `/abe:retro`, `/abe:improve`, `/abe:prototype`, `/abe:make-card`); it asks first only before turning prod on and before starting more than one cloud run at once. With none yet ⏎ starts it. It keeps several **conversations**, each its own session, as a slim tab row under its header (oldest left; a working one dotted, ◆ only on a question or a dialog): a click shows one, `+` (or ⌘⏎ New conversation) starts another beside the others, × closes one (its session stopped, never asked); each is named by your first message to it (`Abe 2` until then). Its ⌘⏎ has Stop… and Recent conversations… (the last 20 closed, by name and when; one picked reopens as a tab); Sessions lists the open ones on top. During `:install` a dim **install strip** sits under its header: `Install 3 of 7`, a mark per part (a click unfolds its last check with Re-check and Do again) and `Files N` (a click lists the files the install wrote, a click opens one in the Editor); Done, `✓ Done` and the files. `/abe:prototype <idea>` (or a card's `Prototype…`) starts a **prototype session**: throwaway code on a `proto/…` branch that is never pushed, the app run from it (on this machine, or on a box under `serve.target: cloud`), opened as its own chat beside the Abe chat: `Prototype · <its words>` (a card's: `Prototype · #4 <title>`) over the preview's address (a click opens the page in the browser), where it runs, its branch and `Starting the app…` until it is up. Ask it to make a card: a New card tagged `prototype`, the prototype's diff and screenshots kept as its reference for the card's run. Terminal mode is the same session's **Claude Code** TUI, the session Chat is pinned to attached (`abe session attach <id>`, a box's `abe box attach <box> --session <id>`, as the Board's ⌃⌘→), following Chat to a Retry's or Resume's new session; Chat and Terminal are one session, two looks. ⌃⌘← / ⌃⌘→ are the column's Chat / Claude Code while it has the keys and on the places and pages without tabs (the Board, Sessions, Boxes, Pipelines, Run, Card); on Code with the keys in the editor, the tree, the Terminal or Git they are that pane's previous / next tab (⌘4 first, or View › Chat Mode / Claude Code, which are the column's wherever the keys are). The column is the AI chat only: shells live in Code's bottom panel, its **Terminal** (⌘T). A hidden Chat keeps a ◆ on the toggle while its session needs you (a question card, a dialog, a run's Needs you; a turn that simply ended is `○ Idle`, no ◆). Each tile keeps its own Chat: docked, maximized (⌘⇧2: Chat fills the middle, the rail and the header stay; `Maximized · ⌘⇧2 docks it`) or hidden (⌘⌥2, again shows it as it was: maximized too), Chat or Claude Code, and its width, across launches (`<state>/vimstorm/rail-memory.json`); a tile never left shows it docked in Chat mode (hidden in a window under 900 px). Each workspace keeps the session its Chat was on (`workspace-chats.json`); on the Board Chat follows the selected card; on Boxes and Pipelines it is the Abe chat, wherever you came from. Chat's draft and transcript scroll are kept per session while the app runs (not across a restart). ⌘⇧D tears it into a window of its own (`Chat · <story>`, `Dock ⌘⇧D puts it back` at its foot; the editor gets the room back); ⌘⇧D in either window, or closing it, docks it again. A torn-out Chat is the window's, not a tile's.

**Boxes** and **Pipelines**, the places under the Board, fill the middle with Abe's lists (from `abe ui bridge`), the Abe chat beside them: Boxes your boxes, Pipelines the runs of the branch of the workspace you were in (the repo's from Abe). **Sessions** is a tab on the Board (Mine · Team · Sessions), for reading, not for moving. A list shown again shows its last rows at once while it reads them anew. Attention is quiet: background Needs you, failures and Ready for review change only the tiles' dots; no banners, bells or dock badge. The wheel over Sessions or Boxes scrolls their rows; the selection stays until a key or a click moves it. Under the code sits Code's bottom panel (Terminal · Git · Problems; ⇧⌘M Problems under the Feel vscode). Settings is the toolbar's gear (a dot while it lists problems). The status bar no longer counts who needs you, what runs or your boxes.

## Workspaces and agents

**+** on the rail, or **File › New Workspace…**, shows the Board on its **New card** dialog: work starts from a card and its Launch box. Every open workspace is a tile on the rail until you close it (nothing hides one); Stop the session…, Delete Working Files… and Close the workspace, its tile's menu, are three things. **Delete Working Files…** lists the uncommitted files and the commits no remote has (Also delete branch, off); Cancel is the default, `d` deletes.

The toolbar's centre is the shown workspace's **context line** (on its pages, Run and Card too): small badges in their hues, the story (its title, cut after a whole word with `…` when long), its stage and state with the time, where it runs and its PR (`#2 cart total ignores… · Build ● Running 4 min · Local · PR —`); on the Board, Boxes and Pipelines `Abe`, the project, the sprint, the ones that need you and those running. A click opens **Linked** as ⌃L does.

## Native pull request review

A PR is reviewed in the Git window's **Log** on the PR's own checked-out worktree: the Log's **PR ⌄** chip lists the repo's open PRs (Mine, then Others with Review requested) and ⏎ checks one out and filters the Log to it (`PR: #n ×`; × clears it, the worktree stays). The PR strip over the Log shows its state, checks and threads, the scope, **Approve…**, **Request changes…**, **Comment…**, Open in browser and **Submit review (N)**. `,ic` drafts a comment on a line or selected range; `,ip` explicitly posts it now. `,ia`, `,ir` and `,is` open the **Submit review** dialog on that verdict (summary required for Request changes; the drafts listed go with it, on the head you read).

In the PR's diff, posted threads and your drafts show as cards under their lines (both panes keep the room): a thread with Reply… and Resolve / Reopen, a draft with Edit draft, Post comment now and Delete… (asked first), an outdated draft (written on an earlier head, never moved) with Rewrite on line N and Delete…; Submit review waits while one is outdated. The toolbar shows `#n`, the scope switch (All changes or one commit, those leaving the file alone grey), ‹ › and the file's **Reviewed** tick. `,it` opens posted discussions and `,iu` visits the next unresolved one. Replies and resolve/reopen follow GitHub permissions; your own PR takes a comment only. Drafts retain their original revision; changed heads and uncertain responses preserve pending work and reconcile before resubmission. **Draft PR…** in the Git header opens the **Create Draft PR** dialog: title, `base ← branch` with its commits and files (the base a pick of origin's branches, the default first: Space / ↓ opens it), a description from the commits and the card, **Reviewers** (Space / ↓ or a click: the repo's people; ⌫ or × takes one off; asked once the draft is made), what is pushed first and what stays local; an open PR for the branch is reused, and while the branch has one, Draft PR… is grey and says which. Mark ready, close and merge (a risky confirm, Cancel the default) are in the Log's menu; agents never merge. Merge stays the Log menu's confirm; no command merges.

## Workflows and skills

**Workflows are files in the repo** (ADR 0033), read and changed in the Editor like the code. The install's Workflows part copies Abe's into the repo's `abe/` folder once: `abe/workflows/<id>.yaml` (Feature `on default`, Quick fix `on type Task`, Bug `on type Bug`, Investigate picked by hand) and `abe/skills/<name>/` (Abe's skills, loaded as `/abe:<name>`; the Abe chat's guide and the run's driver stay Abe's own); from then on they are the repo's, the team's own workflows beside them in `abe/workflows/`, committed like any change. The repo's own Claude Code skills stay in `.claude/skills/`; yours are `.abe/workflows/<id>.local.yaml` in the main checkout (kept out of git). Of one id the nearest wins: Yours over the repo's. A newer Abe never writes over them: its Workflows part says `Abe x.y has newer <names>`, and the Abe chat shows the diff and applies it when you ask. In a repo's main checkout the **Project tree** shows `abe/` first, with Abe's head, and each workflow file carries a dim tag after its name: `Repo’s · on default`, `Repo’s · on type Bug`, `Yours · on tags spike`, red with its problem count while it fails the load check; `:workflows` (Command mode's Anywhere) opens the tree there. A workflow file opens in the Editor with its **preview** on the right (Editor and Preview, `,m` cycles like markdown): its name with whose it is and what it takes, then one card per step top to bottom (its name and a tag, `⟲ fresh` or `on loop`; its main skill, `serve` for checks, and what it does), a gated step dashed with its judge's question and a dashed `skipped if no` around it, a way out as a dashed tag off its card (`no repro → report, end`), its loops as brackets on the right (`↩ build ×10`). The caret's step is ringed; a click on a card puts the caret on its line. The load check runs on open, as you type and on save: a problem gets the red wave at its line, a red banner over the cards (`✗ 1 problem · not loadable`) and its message under the step's card; no card gets the workflow until it loads. ⌘B (gd) on a step goes to its skill's `SKILL.md` (`abe/skills/` or `.claude/skills/`); ⌘B in a `SKILL.md` lists the workflow steps that run it. A skill's `SKILL.md` opens with its page beside it: whose it is, its files as one strip of links (each opens in the Editor), then its text. A change in words is the Abe chat's: ask it (`:chat`), it edits the files and checks them with `abe workflow read`. A card runs the workflow its Launch box picks, preselected by `on:` (else Feature); the run names it (`a Feature run`). A step's `when:` gate holds for its first pass only: a loop back to it always runs it. To fix a teammate's pull request, the pr step says `into: audit.head` (`pr: { skill: pr, fresh: true, into: audit.head }`), and an earlier step reports their head branch (`--fact audit.head=feature/cart-badge`): the card branch starts from it, and its pull request goes from `card/<card>` into their branch, opening ``Fixes onto `feature/cart-badge`.``, never pushed onto theirs and never auto merged (`abe/packages/core/workflows/workflow-yaml.md`).

The preview's head links what can be done with the file (Change with AI ⌘G, Open my copy, Compare, Propose…, Retro…, a retro finding's fix). Its problems get the red wave; `⌥⏎` on such a line lists Abe's fixes first, then More actions… (the LSP's). Only Abe's own, in a repo not installed yet, is read-only (an edit says Read-only here: Edit my copy). `⌘G` in it is Change with AI (Abe's own read-only one: into your copy, made first): the changed lines lit until you save, `⌘S` keeps, `u` undoes. `⌘D` on your copy (or on a Layer's file you have a copy of) is Compare: the two side by side in the diff viewer, the other Layer's left; Edit / Open My Copy drops the read-only original from the tabs. Propose in local mode is held on this machine (`abe local status` lists it). The right-click menu (and `⌘⏎`, `:`) adds Change with AI…, Edit / Open My Copy, Compare with <Layer>, Propose a Change to <Layer>… and Retro….
