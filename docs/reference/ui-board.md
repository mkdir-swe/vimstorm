# The Board's screens

The Board, a card's actions, the Launch box, the Sessions, Boxes and Pipelines tabs and Command mode, as `abe ui bridge` draws them ([the bridge](ui-bridge.md)).

- **The Board** (`factory`, the Factory view's main screen;, 2.1: 2.0's Cards / Flow bands, lanes, History and session cards are retired): `ui/src/board/factory.ts` (the screen), `ui/src/board/flow.ts` (the rules as tables: a stage's place on the stage rail (`RAIL_STAGE_OF`), a run's look (`lookOf`: Starting, the step word (`Running`, `Checking`, `Reviewing`: the same on the rail row as in Chat's header), Needs you, Failed, Stopped (`○`, dim, counted nowhere: you stopped its session, `SessionData.stopped`), Ready for review, Idle, Queued, On test, Abandoned (`○`, dim, its rail on the stage it was given up in, `railStageOf`, and Chat's divider `PR → Abandoned`: never Done or ✓), each with its glyph; a session's own look with no run, `sessionLook`, Queued, Starting, Running, Needs you, Stopped, Ended, the Board card's note (a live session with no run in place of `No session`, or a plain one you stopped, `standsAlone`: `○ Stopped` on the card, in Chat and in Sessions until a newer session on its card; ⏎ / Reopen goes back into it; a Factory run's session, `SessionData.factory`, never stands alone once its run is cleared) and Chat's header alike), what stands out (Needs you, Failed), a card's rail row (`railRow`), the one clock beside a state word on every screen (`clockSince`: while the session waits on you since it began to wait, else since the run's stay on its rail stage began, `stayStart`: build and checks are one Build stay), its box mark (`boxMarkOf`: the session's box, else the one tagged with the card (`abe:card`) or Queued for it; its state from `box.state` since the list was read, else `box list`, else the session), whose card on Team, the counts and widgets), `ui/src/board/board.ts` (its components: BoardHeader, BoardColumns, BoardColumn, BoardCard with its rail row; the column rules `columnLayout` / `firstShown`; Rust `app/src/factory/board.rs`). The team's Azure columns (`card list --mine --closed` / `card list --closed`, Mine · Team: the open cards and the current sprint's done ones, else the last 14 days' with no sprint; a done card's title dim, its run's rail row or `✓ Done`; Removed never), each the same width whatever it holds (200 px at the least, so all four fit at 1400 px with Chat open; else as many as fit share the width whole, none cut at the edge, scrolled sideways by whole columns keeping the selected column in view, with a thumb at the bottom and those out of view named at the header's right, `‹ New 2 · Closed 0 ›`; a card at most 360 px, left-aligned). While a card list is read for the first time the columns count nothing and the first says `Reading the board…`; read again (`r`, the clock after a failure) the header says `Reading…` until it answers; every card: `#id`, type, initials (a round badge); the title in two lines at most; its rail row (segments, stage word, state glyph and word in its hue, ` · 21 min`, the box mark) or `No session`, one line at any width (words left out whole, the stage word first, then the box mark's; then the segments narrow; the state's word cut last with an ellipsis); its tooltip below it, only when it says what the card does not (`tipAdds`: a reason; or a title or word the shell cut). After opening it reads nothing whole again but `box list` (quietly every 5 min, `ui/src/live/box-feed.ts`) and a card list that failed (`Board not read · …`; live's `cardsRetried` in `ui/src/live/facts.ts`, for every screen showing cards: read again 15 s after the clock first sees it, the wait doubling up to 2 min, also while the Editor is shown; the Board reads it at once when opened again (⌘⌥A from the Editor, `:factory`), so the header clears by itself soon after Azure DevOps answers; `r` and another project start the wait anew). Otherwise `factory.stage` (carrying the run's pull request as `factory status` says it, core's `stageEventOf`, so every screen's `lookOf` says Ready for review at once, and how a person ended it, `ended`, so an abandoned run stays on the stage it was given up in on the card, Chat's header and Sessions at once), `session.state`, `session.subagent`, `box.state` and `box.idle` change cards and boxes in place (a box not known reads `box list` again); an unknown run, a moved pull request or a run abandoned by an older box's serve (no `ended`: `endUnsaid`) is read by its card (`factory status <card>`), an unknown session by its id (`session show <id>`). `ui/src/live/live.ts` is how the Board, Chat and Sessions keep runs and sessions current: `runMoved` / `sessionMoved` / `subagentMoved` apply an event in place, and a read's answer is merged with `freshRuns` / `freshSessions`, which keep what moved after the read was asked (`hearing` notes each event's and each read's `seq`) or began later: a slow read (a cloud run's, its box asked) never takes a run or session back. Keys: `k ä` columns, one press the next column with cards (an empty one is no stop, passed over; nothing further, it stays), a count that many columns with cards, `l ö` cards, `gg` `G`, Tab Mine ↔ Team, `/` filter (⏎ keeps it, Esc clears), ⏎ the story (the card dialog; never a launch), ⌘⏎ the card's menu. The first card on open: the oldest that needs you, else your first in board order, else the first; the card under the cursor is the state's `selected` (Chat follows it), a card read again keeps its place in its column while the screen is open (`keptOrder`: the board lists the newest change first, so an assigned or commented card would jump from under the mouse; a card new to the list or to its column, moved, comes first, as the board has it), held by its id whatever moves (a read or an event reordering the columns, the keyboard leaving and coming back, a restarted bridge, which the shell opens on the card and tab it last drew: `factory {card, tab}`; a `factory {card}` with no tab, Sessions' ⏎ too, shows Team when only Team holds the card: `seek`). While the pointer is over the Board the shell holds back a tree of the same screen and selection that moves a drawn card (`HOLD`, 1.5 s; the pointer leaving, the view hidden, the window to the back, a key or a click shows it at once, and what comes within `INPUT_ANSWER` (1 s) of a key or click is never held), so a click lands on the card aimed at. A Sheet tab and a local pull request take the Board's place until Esc. `views` tweaks naming a retired part (`lane:History`, `flow`) are ignored and dropped on the next save of their Layer (`isRetiredTweak`). The contract's `replay/factory/` holds its trees after each of a few keys, which the shell's fake bridge answers keys with.
